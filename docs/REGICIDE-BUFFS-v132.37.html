They would all have shipped silent

SND_DATA is the audio payload; DEFS is the registry — and the loader and resolve() both key on DEFS. Twelve files were embedded and twelve Sound.play calls were wired, every existing gate was green, and nothing would have made a noise. No error, no log, just a 350 KB heavier bundle. Registering them is the fix; the smoketest now asserts DEFS ≡ SND_DATA in both directions so it cannot recur.

The second half was worse: ten of the twelve fire from host-only code, so a guest heard two of twelve — and a dedicated server, which has no local player at all, would have played the whole set to an empty room. All twelve now go on the wire, positionally, host-throttled at half the client's window.
