Seven of the twelve were sounds the game already played

The v132.37 gate that claimed “no sound reuse” compared keys. It was green while bleedhit was gore1, wardblock was arrowhit1, stunhit was hit1 — the same recording, different filename, re-encoded so even the bytes differed. A player does not hear a key.

A new screen decodes every sound in the bank and compares all 11,935 pairs by envelope and spectrum. It found those seven, and one that predates them: bighaul, cannonhit and alert_attack had been three keys on one recording since v104 — a good trade payout sounded exactly like a cannonball landing on you.

The fix is John’s: combine it with another sound to make it unique. Eleven cues are now baked composites — two or three library sounds mixed at build time with per-layer gain, delay and a real resample. Baked rather than played twice, so each costs one voice, one decode, one panned source. Every ingredient still scores 1.000 against something in the bank; every composite drops to 0.78–0.93 with a different nearest neighbour. Best of them is Rapid Volley — three bow twangs at 115ms intervals, because that is literally what the buff does.
