📜 HOW TO PLAY

⚔ The Goal — Regicide

Two kingdoms of 50. Kill the enemy King and the war is won (+500 pts to the slayer). If yours falls first, it's over — castles, walls and garrisons exist to keep him breathing. No priest can heal a king.

🥾 You Are ONE Soldier

WASD move · mouse steers the camera · LMB attack · wheel zoom. You're a single body among ~110 — the rest are AI (or your friends). Die and you respawn as a Villager at your Town Center (V switches spawn to a forward castle; houses shorten the wait; your body lies where it fell).

🌾 Economy — the Shared Warchest

Walk to berries, trees, gold or stone and hold E to gather (carry 20, deposit at the Town Center or a Storage Pit). Everything feeds ONE team stockpile that pays for troops, buildings and ages. Farms grow corn — work them or harvest ripe crop with E. Stone is scarce: five piles on the whole map. Wood comes from chopping forests.

⏳ The Six Ages

Stone → Bronze → Iron → Classical → Medieval → Enlightenment. Bank food and press T at your Town Center to advance. Each age unlocks buildings and upgrades every unit line's tier — and later-age troops are simply hardier.

🏗 Building

B opens the build menu; place a foundation and hold E to swing the hammer. Houses speed respawns · Barracks/Archery Range/Stable/Temple/Siege Workshop train the lines · Walls & gates (yours open for you) · Watch/guard towers — climb in with E and shoot from the deck · The Castle: forward spawn, drop-off, and a rain of arrows.

🗡 Arms & the Wheel

Press R at a training building to re-arm (team resources pay). The wheel: anti-cavalry skewers horses ×3 · ranged outshoots spears · melee & cavalry run down archers. Melee: hold RMB to block, tap it as a blow lands to PARRY. Ranged: hold RMB to aim, LMB for a hard skill shot. Catapults/trebuchets lob over walls with an aim ring — and their splash hits armies at FULL force, though arrows barely scratch the engines themselves (swords dismantle them). Cavalry dominates everything but spears. Dragoons carry a six-shot pistol (RMB, no refills until you re-arm); the Musketeer fixes a BAYONET for anything at arm's length.

⚑ Command — Rally & CHARGE

G rallies your five nearest AI soldiers to your side (the Bannerman blacksmith buff adds one per stack — up to eight). Then F sounds the CHARGE: they attack-move down your gaze — slaying units, razing buildings, storming even creep camps — and then HOLD that ground until you recall them with G or re-aim with F.

✝ The Priest

Trained at the Temple (Classical age). His aura mends nearby allies — and he can raise the dead: hold LMB ~2s to channel (the orb turns gold), stand over a fallen ally, release to resurrect them at full health, keeping the class they died as. Ten seconds of faith between miracles. Cold iron stays dead — no siege engines.

📜 Quests & the Blacksmith

A Town Board stands beside each Town Center — press E there for a random quest (25 different postings; E twice rerolls one you hate, on a 60s cooldown). Completing a quest earns +1 Level and +1 XP — the hardest pay 2. From the Iron Age, spend XP at the Blacksmith (100 wood, one per team, the AI builds one too) with E: the smith lays out THREE random buffs — you choose ONE (1 XP; the same three wait until you pick, so no reroll-fishing) — sharper steel, thicker hide, faster feet, crits, lifesteal — stacking up to ×3 of a kind. Your Level shines over your head for friend and foe alike. Death wipes your level, XP and every buff — glory is rented, never owned. Max level 20.

🐴 Trade — the Kings Road

Three neutral bazaars sit along the road between the thrones, evenly placed for both teams. Markets (max 5) send AI carts to haul gold — the deeper the route, the richer. Arm as a Trader to run routes yourself at 4× the pay. Scout-line cavalry trample farms and hunt carts: escort yours, plunder theirs.

🐺 The Wilds — Creep Camps

Six camps hide in pockets beyond the map's edge (corners + long-edge midpoints). Packs of 4-5 wolves guard a 300 FOOD chest; barbarians guard 300 GOLD. They leash to their camps and heal when left alone — and they will tear a lone soldier apart, so bring 2-3 allies. First boot on the chest takes it, enemy boots included. A new pack rises 3 minutes after a wipe and drags off unclaimed treasure.

🛶 The Viking Shore

A wrecked longship rots on a beach past the southern edge. At 15:00 a raid lands: a giant Viking Chieftain and ten raiders. Break the whole raid with a war party and TWIN chests drop — 500 food AND 500 gold — each stealable separately. The next raid lands 15 minutes later and sweeps whatever nobody claimed.

🏆 Scoring

Deposits pay 1 pt per resource · kills pay the victim's cost (villagers 10) · healing pays per HP mended · resurrections 25 · chests pay their value · the regicide itself: 500. Hold TAB for the scoreboard.

🧠 The AI Marshals

Every AI team rolls a doctrine at the horns — your scouts report it a minute in. RUSH throws early, relentless waves and hunts your villagers, carts and King. BOOM farms wide and races for the Enlightenment before crushing you with elite tiers. TURTLE raises walls, towers and twin castles, bleeds your attacks, then counterpunches with everything at once. EXPANSIONIST sprawls — pits, markets and endless fields. In SOLO and CO-OP the enemy AI has an 🌱 EASY / 🔥 HARD dial: Hard thinks faster, counters your army's composition using the wheel, and runs its economy 20% hot.

🌐 Multiplayer

The host runs the war; guests' bodies answer instantly and the host's word is law. Hosts choose ⚔ PvP (joiners pick BLUE or RED) or 🤝 Co-Op vs AI (every joiner fights for BLUE against the Red AI), and open a 🌐 public hall — visible in the JOIN screen's 🔭 server browser — or a 🔒 private one, unlisted and guarded by a password (share the room code in Discord). Everyone must run the SAME version — hard-refresh after updates.

SLAIN

You respawn as a Villager at your Town Center. Houses shorten this wait. Your gear is lost — re-arm at the Barracks (team resources). Your level, XP and blacksmith buffs die with you.
